# Discover Forge Whenever the Bridge Appears

Forge Expansion 0.15.6 removes the stage requirement from Forge discovery. Whenever your journey reaches Celestium's Ethereal Bridge, the engineering relay is ready to find and use.

Explore the west-side service bay off the left-hand route and approach its lever to unlock Forge for your current party. The relay keeps its fixed location, animation and portrait notification. Existing unlock progress carries forward.

Both Windows and Linux kits include the revised gameplay. Update your co-op group together to version 0.15.6.
