# Ascendant: Grow Stronger Between Stages

Build momentum before the next challenge. Ascendant now captures your team's build strength and average character level when a combat stage loads, then holds its health factor and extra-elite chance steady throughout that stage. Your upgrades pay off immediately; the next stage is where the opposition adapts.

The opening stage has no Ascendant bonus. Through character level 30, Ascendant's added health bonus and extra-elite chance are reduced by 10%. This easing gradually fades over levels 31–50, returning exactly to the established formula at level 50. The end-game curve and caps remain intact.

Solo uses your own build and level. Co-op uses team averages captured by the host; deaths, joins and item purchases do not change the current stage's snapshot. Continue retains the same stage-entry snapshot. Native difficulty and separately selected Aspects keep their independent behavior.
