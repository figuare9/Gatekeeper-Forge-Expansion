# Aspects Ready for Solo Play

Chaos, Scaling and Elite now register before the normal Single Player Aspects menu builds its cells. No private game is needed to make the new Aspects appear. Existing menus add missing cells before setup without duplicates, and solo play does not wait for remote compatibility checks.

The Elite Aspect also gains a recovery check for ordinary enemies that reach zero health but fail to complete their death confirmation. The host allows a short grace period for the normal message, then uses the game's native death, reward and encounter handling. This preserves kill attribution and avoids repeated rewards. Living enemies, scripted boss phases and training dummies are excluded.

Forge Expansion 0.15.8 includes matching Windows and Linux kits. Ascendant's stage pacing, Aspect strengths and character balance are unchanged. Update co-op groups together.
